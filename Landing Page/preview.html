A little more room to notice

Made for the moments that matter.

Every day brings new opportunities to notice, encourage, and support a child’s learning. VikasSetu keeps the useful context close, while you stay in the lead.

01

Understand every child

Keep child profiles and recorded observations together, so the small details that matter stay close at hand.

02

Keep the classroom organised

Bring classroom information and attendance into one calm, practical place for the day-to-day work.

03

Find meaningful activities

When relevant child information is available, activity suggestions can help you choose a thoughtful next step.

04

Notice progress over time

Use observations and activity-trial notes to reflect on what a child is exploring and where to go next.

A wider view of learning

Many ways to learn. Every child’s own journey.

Learning is not one straight line. These five familiar paths make it easier to notice what is inviting a child in today.

Alphabet & Language

Build a love of words through sounds, letters, conversations, and early mark-making.

Numbers & Counting

Make quantity visible with counting, matching, comparing, and everyday number talk.

Colours & Shapes

Notice patterns, colours, lines, and shapes as children make sense of the world around them.

Art & Creativity

Leave room for experimenting with materials, movement, mark-making, and imagination.

Stories, Habits & Games

Grow language, routines, social learning, and joy through stories, songs, and shared play.

A gentle rhythm for the day

Small observations. Thoughtful next steps.

VikasSetu is a supportive tool—not a replacement for your judgement. You notice, choose, and reflect with the information available to you.

01

Observe

Notice a child’s interest, activity, or learning behaviour and record an observation where the system supports it.

02

Choose an activity

Use the available context to decide on an activity that feels relevant, playful, and right for the moment.

03

Reflect on progress

Record activity-trial information where supported and let your next observation grow from what you see.
